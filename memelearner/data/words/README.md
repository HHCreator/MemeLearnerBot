`common_english.txt` is the ~10,000 most common English words (US, no swear words) from
https://github.com/first20hours/google-10000-english, based on Google's Trillion Word Corpus.
surf.py uses it to tell English words (nice) from Latin-letter memes (yyds), the same list
the translator (StupidTransilator) uses to split compound words.
